# Leave Policy

Company: Northstar Holdings
Document Status: Canonical
Last Updated: 2026-09-01

## Purpose
This document defines the rules for annual leave, sick leave, unpaid leave, and leave approval.

## Annual Leave
Employees become eligible to request annual leave after completing 90 days of employment.
Eligible employees receive 24 annual leave days per year.
Annual leave requests must normally be submitted at least 5 calendar days before the requested start date.

## Annual Leave Approval
Annual leave follows this approval sequence:
1. Direct Manager
2. HR
The Direct Manager reviews operational availability. HR performs the final policy and balance review.
A leave request is considered approved only after both required approvals are completed.

## Leave Cancellation
An employee may cancel a leave request while it is still pending approval.
Once fully approved, the employee cannot cancel it directly.
An approved leave request may only be cancelled by HR Admin. HR Admin must provide a cancellation reason.

## Sick Leave
Employees may submit sick leave without prior notice when the illness is unexpected.
If sick leave is longer than 2 consecutive calendar days, a medical certificate is required.
Sick leave is reviewed by HR.

## Unpaid Leave
Unpaid leave requires Direct Manager approval and HR approval.
Unpaid leave does not use the employee's annual leave balance.

## Important Rules
An employee must not be allowed to approve their own leave request.
A Direct Manager may approve leave only for employees assigned to them.
HR approval does not replace Direct Manager approval unless an exceptional process is explicitly defined by company policy.
