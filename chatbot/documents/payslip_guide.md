# Payslip Guide

Company: Northstar Holdings
Document Status: Canonical
Last Updated: 2026-09-01

## Purpose
This document explains how employees access their monthly payslips and who may view payroll information.

## Employee Access
Employees can view and download only their own payslips.
Payslips are available in Employee Portal → Payroll → Payslips.
Payslips are published after the monthly payroll process has been finalized.
The target publication date is the 28th day of each month.
If payroll has not yet been finalized, the payslip may not be available.

## Payslip Format
Payslips are available as PDF files.
A payslip may contain employee name, employee number, payroll month, basic salary, allowances, deductions, overtime, and net salary.

## Manager Access
Direct Managers and Project Managers do not automatically have access to employee payslips.
Manager authority over an employee does not grant payroll visibility.

## HR Access
Authorized Payroll HR users may view employee payslips when required for payroll operations or employee support.
General HR access does not automatically mean payroll access.

## Security
Payslip access must be protected by backend authorization.
A user must not gain access to another employee's payslip by changing an employee ID, URL parameter, or request body.
Payslip download links must not be treated as authorization.

## Missing Payslip
If an employee's expected payslip is not available after payroll publication, the employee should contact Payroll HR.
