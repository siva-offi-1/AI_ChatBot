# Attendance Policy

Company: Northstar Holdings
Document Status: Canonical
Last Updated: 2026-09-01

## Office Hours
Office hours are 08:00 AM to 05:00 PM, with a one-hour break.

## Grace Period and Late Arrival
A 10-minute grace period is allowed. Arrival after 08:10 AM is considered late.
Three late arrivals in a calendar month require HR review.

## Salary Deduction
Late arrival does not automatically cause salary deduction. Payroll policy determines any consequences.

## Missing Punch Request
Employees must submit a Missing Punch Request within 3 business days.
Approval sequence: Direct Manager, then HR.
Late requests may be rejected unless HR accepts an exceptional reason.

## Attendance Recording
Office fingerprint devices are used. Approved remote locations may use mobile attendance when enabled.

## Attendance Visibility
Employees may view their own attendance. Direct Managers may view attendance for employees assigned to them.

## Missing Attendance Data
Missing attendance data must not automatically be treated as absence. It must be reviewed and resolved first.
