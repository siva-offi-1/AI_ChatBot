# Flight Ticket Policy

Company: Northstar Holdings
Document Status: Canonical
Last Updated: 2026-09-01

## Purpose
This document defines employee eligibility and approval rules for company-provided annual flight tickets.

## Eligibility
An employee becomes eligible for an annual company flight ticket after completing 11 months of continuous employment.
The employee must have active employment status when submitting the request.

## Request Process
Eligible employees can submit a flight ticket request from Employee Portal → Travel → Flight Tickets.
The employee selects preferred travel date, destination, ticket type, and relevant notes.

## Approval
Flight ticket requests follow this approval sequence:
1. HR eligibility review
2. HR travel processing
The Direct Manager is not part of the standard flight ticket approval workflow.

## Booking
Depending on company configuration, HR books the ticket for the employee, or the employee purchases the ticket and requests reimbursement.
The booking method must be shown to the employee before final confirmation.

## Ineligible Requests
If the employee has completed less than 11 months of continuous employment, the normal annual flight ticket request must be rejected as not yet eligible.
Exceptions require a separate HR exception process. This document does not define the exception approval workflow.

## Security
Employees may view only their own flight ticket requests.
HR users processing travel requests must access requests through their authorized HR workflow.

## Important Limitation
This document does not define business-class eligibility, family ticket eligibility, international travel insurance, or visa approval rules.
For these topics, the system should state that the available documentation does not provide the answer.
